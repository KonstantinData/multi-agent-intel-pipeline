# Deep Research Quality Rubric

Scoring rubric for evaluating Liquisto pre-meeting briefing quality.
Derived from the deep-research-report quality anchor.

Each dimension is scored 0–3:
- **0** = absent or placeholder only
- **1** = present but generic / unsourced / incomplete
- **2** = adequate with some sourced evidence and specificity
- **3** = strong, sourced, specific, and meeting-actionable

A briefing is **meeting-ready** when the total score is ≥ 14/21 AND no
single dimension scores 0.

---

## Dimensions

### D1: Inventory / Working-Capital Signals (0–3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No inventory or working-capital signals mentioned |
| 1 | Generic industry-level statements without company-specific evidence |
| 2 | At least one company-specific signal with source reference |
| 3 | Multiple dated, sourced signals with explicit absence statements for gaps |

### D2: Contact Intelligence (0–3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No contacts identified |
| 1 | Fewer than 3 contacts or no outreach angles |
| 2 | ≥ 3 contacts at ≥ 2 firms with role and seniority |
| 3 | ≥ 3 contacts with role, seniority, outreach angle, and coverage quality assessed |

### D3: Opportunity Prioritization (0–3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No Liquisto service path assessment |
| 1 | All paths marked "unclear" or generic one-liner |
| 2 | At least one path with evidence-based reasoning |
| 3 | All three paths assessed with clear evidence-based ranking |

### D4: Buyer / Redeployment Specificity (0–3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No buyers or redeployment paths identified |
| 1 | Generic "resale possible" without named buyers |
| 2 | ≥ 1 named buyer with relevance explanation |
| 3 | ≥ 3 named buyers tied to specific product categories with relevance |

### D5: Fact vs. Inference Separation (0–3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No source references anywhere |
| 1 | Some sources listed but claims not individually traceable |
| 2 | Most claims traceable to sources; inferences occasionally unlabeled |
| 3 | All claims sourced or explicitly labeled as inference; no unsupported claims |

### D6: Meeting-Action Usefulness (0–3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No actionable items |
| 1 | Generic "further research needed" items only |
| 2 | At least one concrete meeting action with evidence reference |
| 3 | ≥ 3 concrete actions referencing specific evidence or gaps |

### D7: Economic / Commercial Situation (0–3)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 0 | No economic assessment |
| 1 | Generic assessment without events or pressure signals |
| 2 | Assessment with at least one sourced recent event |
| 3 | Assessment with multiple sourced events, pressure classification, and trend |

---

## Thresholds

| Outcome | Condition |
|---------|-----------|
| Meeting-ready | Total ≥ 14/21 AND no dimension = 0 |
| Usable with gaps | Total ≥ 10/21 AND D1+D3+D4 ≥ 4 |
| Not usable | Total < 10 OR any core dimension (D1, D3, D4) = 0 |
