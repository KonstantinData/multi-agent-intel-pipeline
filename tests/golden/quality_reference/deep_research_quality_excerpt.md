# Deep Research Quality Excerpt

This excerpt defines the **target end-product quality** for Liquisto
pre-meeting briefings. It is derived from the deep-research-report quality
anchor referenced in `refactor-architecture-final.md`.

---

## What a meeting-ready briefing must demonstrate

### 1. Inventory / Working-Capital Signal Detection
- Concrete signals of excess stock, write-downs, plant closures, workforce
  reductions, or supply-demand imbalance — not generic industry commentary.
- Signals must be sourced and dated where possible.
- Absence of signals must be stated explicitly ("no public inventory
  pressure signals found"), not silently omitted.

### 2. Contact Intelligence
- Named decision-makers at prioritized buyer firms with role, seniority,
  and a concrete outreach angle per contact.
- Coverage quality assessed (high / medium / low) with reasoning.
- At least 3 contacts across at least 2 buyer firms for a meeting-ready
  briefing.

### 3. Opportunity Prioritization
- Clear ranking of Liquisto service paths (excess inventory monetization,
  repurposing/circularity, analytics/decision support) with evidence-based
  reasoning for each.
- "Unclear" is an acceptable assessment when evidence is genuinely absent,
  but must not be the default for all paths.

### 4. Buyer / Redeployment Specificity
- Named buyer companies with relevance explanation per buyer.
- Monetization and redeployment paths tied to specific product categories
  or asset types, not generic "resale possible" statements.
- Downstream buyers distinguished from peer competitors.

### 5. Fact vs. Inference Separation
- Every claim must be traceable to a source (URL, page excerpt, or
  explicit "inferred from [evidence]").
- Inferences must be labeled as such.
- Unsupported claims must not appear in the final briefing.

### 6. Meeting-Action Usefulness
- Final output must contain concrete, actionable meeting preparation items:
  what to ask, what to validate, what to propose.
- Generic "further research needed" is not a meeting action.
- Each action must reference the evidence or gap that motivates it.
